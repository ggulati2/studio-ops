AutoCheck watches what QC does with its [Hygiene] comments on Frame.io and tunes itself, per agency and film type. Nobody has to click anything.

AutoCheck rules
How it learns
  • Wrong flag: QC deletes the [Hygiene] comment on Frame.io, or writes "not an issue" / "intended" next to it.
  • Right flag: QC writes their own note about the same thing next to it, or the editor fixes it in the next version.
  • Missed: QC writes a note about something AutoCheck measures (e.g. "shaky", "out of focus", "audio distorted") where it flagged nothing.
  • It learns when the editor sends the next version. A limit only moves after {{ min_samples }} examples, in small steps, and never switches a check off. Every change is listed below, and you can undo it or lock the limit.
