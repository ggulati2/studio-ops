Red Wed QC Helper

A small free Chrome extension that runs AutoCheck from the QC dashboard: it reads V1's Frame.io comments, checks V2 in HD, and posts [Hygiene] comments on V2. If a [Hygiene] comment is wrong, delete it on Frame.io (or reply "not an issue"): AutoCheck learns from that. Everything runs in your browser.

Install (once, about 2 minutes)

  1. Click Download QC Helper above. You get a .zip file.
  2. Unzip it into a folder you won't delete, for example Documents/RedWed-QC-Helper.
  3. In Chrome open chrome://extensions (in Edge: edge://extensions).
  4. Turn on Developer mode (switch at the top right).
  5. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped RedWed-QC-Helper folder.
  6. Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar and pin Red Wed QC Helper.
  7. Reload the QC dashboard. The AutoCheck card no longer says the helper is missing.
  8. Stay signed in to Frame.io in this Chrome, so it can post comments under your account.

Using it

  1. On the QC dashboard, open the film and find the AutoCheck card.
  2. Paste the V1 link (the version with QC comments) and the V2 link (the fixed version), then click AutoCheck.
  3. A tab opens showing progress; Frame.io tabs open and close by themselves. Keep Chrome open and the computer awake: a full film takes about 10–15 minutes, a trailer a couple of minutes.
  4. When it finishes, the comments are on V2 and the report is on the QC dashboard.

Updating

When the AutoCheck card says QC Helper update available: download the new zip, replace the files in the same folder, then click ↻ (reload) on the QC Helper in chrome://extensions. Chrome may show a notice about developer-mode extensions at start-up; it's normal for private extensions, just close it.
